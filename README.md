# AI-Assisted Video Editing Workflow

> **An AI-assisted video editing workflow to reduce production time and skill barriers for content creators.**

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores how Artificial Intelligence can be integrated into the video production and editing process to make content creation faster, more accessible, and less dependent on advanced technical skills.

The project demonstrates a practical workflow combining AI-powered tools with conventional video editing techniques to create a commercial-style product video.

The project was developed as a final project for the **EoAI (Essentials of AI)** course.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional video production and editing can require considerable time, technical knowledge, and experience.

For beginners and small content creators, creating a professional-looking video may involve several separate tasks, including:

- Developing a concept
- Writing a script
- Creating or sourcing visuals
- Generating video content
- Producing voice-over
- Editing and arranging clips
- Adding transitions and effects
- Producing the final version

The objective of this project is to explore how AI-assisted tools can simplify and accelerate these activities while keeping human creativity and editing decisions at the center of the workflow.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. To develop an AI-assisted video production workflow.
2. To explore the use of AI tools at different stages of video creation.
3. To reduce the time required for video production and editing.
4. To simplify video creation for users with limited editing experience.
5. To demonstrate how AI and human editing skills can work together.
6. To produce a practical commercial-style product video.

---

## 🧠 AI-Assisted Workflow

The project follows this general workflow:

```text
Idea / Concept
      ↓
Script Development
      ↓
AI-Assisted Visual Creation
      ↓
Video Generation
      ↓
Voice / Audio
      ↓
Video Editing
      ↓
Review & Refinement
      ↓
Final Video
```

AI was used as an assistant throughout the workflow rather than as a complete replacement for human decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

The project involved a combination of AI and conventional creative tools.

### AI / Generative Tools

- **ChatGPT** – brainstorming, planning, scripting and content assistance
- **Google Flow** – AI-assisted video generation
- **Kling** – AI video generation
- **Canva** – visual design and creative assets

### Video Editing Tools

- **Descript** – video/audio editing and content refinement
- **CapCut** – final editing, arrangement, transitions and polishing

Different tools were used for different stages of the workflow according to their strengths.

## 🎬 Project Demonstration

The practical demonstration focuses on creating a commercial-style product video.

The workflow included:

**Concept → Product visuals → AI-generated video clips → Voice/audio → Editing → Final commercial video**

The product used for the demonstration was a luxury wallet.

---

## ⏱️ Time-Saving Demonstration

One of the practical aspects of the project was comparing the source material with the edited master.

### Before Editing

**Raw video duration:** approximately 06:11

### After Editing

**Final master duration:** approximately 04:31

The editing process removed unnecessary material, improved the sequence, and created a more focused final presentation.

The purpose of this comparison is not simply to shorten the video, but to demonstrate how an AI-assisted workflow combined with human editing decisions can streamline content production.

## 🔄 Human + AI Collaboration

An important principle demonstrated by this project is that AI can function as a productivity assistant rather than completely replacing the creator.

### AI can assist with:

- Ideas
- Scripts
- Visual concepts
- Image generation
- Video generation
- Voice/audio assistance
- Content refinement

### Human contribution remains important for:

- Creative direction
- Selecting suitable outputs
- Storytelling
- Editing decisions
- Timing
- Quality control
- Brand consistency
- Final approval

The final result therefore represents a **human-directed, AI-assisted workflow**.

## 📈 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates a practical approach to incorporating AI into video production.

The main outcome is a structured workflow in which AI tools assist with multiple stages of production while human judgment remains responsible for selecting, editing, refining and approving the final content.

The project also demonstrates that AI-assisted workflows can make video production more approachable for creators who may not have extensive professional video-editing experience.
